The Studio

SMALL TEAM. SHARP EDGES.

Pineapple Games started in a shared apartment with two laptops and a running joke: every good game needs a little spike in it. Years later, the joke's still the studio's whole philosophy.

We build fast, colorful multiplayer games that reward good instincts over grinding — and we ship them small enough that you can learn the rules in one round.
